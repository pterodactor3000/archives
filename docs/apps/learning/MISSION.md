# Mission: Angular and TypeScript interview readiness

## Why
Prepare for a finance-firm technical interview in four days. Build enough retrieval speed and coding fluency to explain Angular and TypeScript choices, solve likely interview problems, and communicate tradeoffs under time pressure.

## Success looks like
- Explain and implement common Angular patterns without relying on notes
- Write strict, readable TypeScript and defend key type-design choices
- Recognize high-yield algorithm patterns and solve representative problems aloud
- Complete a timed mock interview, identify gaps, and correct them before interview day

## Constraints
- Four days remain
- Five focused study hours are available each day
- Diagnostic baseline is 4/15: knowledge 3/6, TypeScript 1/3, Angular 0/3, algorithms 0/3
- Learner has five years of Angular experience, last used version 16, and ten years of TypeScript experience
- LeetCode experience is a few months, but solutions need stronger dry-run and edge-case discipline
- Expected format is one hour of live coding and one hour of technical discussion for a high-mid or senior role
- Practice must favor active coding and spoken explanation over passive reading
- LeetCode may be part of the interview

## Out of scope
- Assuming every finance firm asks finance-specific algorithms
- Deep framework internals with little interview value
- Broad backend, cloud, and finance-domain study unless later interview evidence makes it relevant
